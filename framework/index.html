The Built to Run Framework

Six pillars for building a business that operates independently of any single person.

1

Systems Thinking

See your business as an interconnected system of inputs, processes, and outputs. Map every workflow and understand how changes in one area affect everything else.

  • Business process mapping
  • Input-output analysis
  • Bottleneck identification
  • Dependency charting
2

Process Documentation

Create standard operating procedures that are clear, maintainable, and actually used by your team. Build a single source of truth for every workflow.

  • SOP writing framework
  • Version control for processes
  • Training integration
  • Continuous improvement loops
3

Strategic Delegation

Move beyond task assignment to true outcome delegation. Build trust, accountability, and ownership at every level of your organization.

  • Four levels of delegation
  • Decision rights framework
  • Accountability structures
  • Escalation protocols
4

Team Architecture

Hire, onboard, and retain people who thrive in a systems-driven environment. Build teams that own their results and improve processes organically.

  • Systems-fit hiring criteria
  • Scalable onboarding
  • Competency milestones
  • Self-managing team design
5

Metrics and Dashboards

Know the health of your business at a glance. Build dashboards that surface the signal and let you manage by exception, not by presence.

  • Leading vs. lagging indicators
  • Dashboard design principles
  • Exception-based management
  • Weekly reporting cadence
6

Automation and Scale

Automate the repeatable, delegate the variable, and eliminate the unnecessary. Scale proven systems without losing quality or control.

  • Automation decision matrix
  • Tool selection framework
  • Integration architecture
  • Scale-readiness checklist

How the Pillars Work Together

The six pillars are not independent. They form a reinforcing loop: systems thinking informs your documentation, documentation enables delegation, delegation requires the right team, the team needs metrics to self-manage, and metrics reveal what to automate. Each pillar strengthens the others.

The book walks you through implementing each pillar in sequence, with templates, worksheets, and real examples at every step.
