About the Book

Everything you need to know about Built to Run and why it was written.

The Problem

Most founders start a business to gain freedom, but end up building a job they cannot leave. They become the bottleneck for every decision, every process, and every customer interaction. The business depends entirely on them.

Built to Run was written to solve that problem. This book provides a complete, step-by-step framework for building business systems that operate independently of the founder, allowing the business to scale, the team to thrive, and the founder to step back without everything falling apart.

Who This Book Is For

  • Founders who feel trapped in the day-to-day operations of their business
  • Business owners ready to scale but unsure how to do it without burning out
  • Operators and COOs looking for a proven systems framework
  • Entrepreneurs preparing for exit who need the business to run without them
  • Anyone building a team and wanting to delegate effectively from day one

What You Will Learn

Systems Architecture

How to map, document, and optimize every process in your business so nothing lives in anyone's head.

SOP Creation

Write standard operating procedures that people actually use, with templates you can deploy immediately.

Strategic Delegation

A framework for handing off ownership of outcomes, not just tasks, to your team.

Hiring for Systems

Recruit, onboard, and retain people who thrive in a systems-driven environment.

Metrics That Matter

Build dashboards and KPIs that tell you the health of your business at a glance.

Automation at Scale

Identify what to automate, what to delegate, and what to eliminate entirely.

Outcomes

By the time you finish Built to Run and implement the framework, you will have:

  • A fully documented operational playbook for your business
  • A delegation system that gives your team true ownership
  • Dashboards that let you manage by exception, not by presence
  • An onboarding process that gets new hires productive in days, not months
  • The freedom to step away knowing the business will keep running
