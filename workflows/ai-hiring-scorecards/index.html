AI role and hiring scorecards by business type

AI role and hiring scorecards workflows for 20 business types, with practical records, prompts, examples and working checklists.

This collection focuses on one decision: define a role before recruiting. The workflow produces a job-relevant role scorecard and interview worksheet. Choose the business context to get its relevant records, worked scenario, review questions and editable checklist.

How this workflow works

Translate the role into observable work outputs and consistent interview questions. Review criteria for relevance to the actual job.

Do not infer personal traits from names, backgrounds or unrelated profile information.
