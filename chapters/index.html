Chapter Overview

15 chapters covering every aspect of building a business that runs without you.

01

The Founder Trap

Why most founders build a business that depends entirely on them, and how to recognize the warning signs before burnout hits.

02

Systems Thinking for Business

How to see your business as an interconnected machine. Map inputs, processes, and outputs so you can optimize the whole, not just the parts.

03

The Operations Audit

A complete methodology for auditing your current operations, identifying bottlenecks, and prioritizing what to systemize first.

04

Writing SOPs That Actually Get Used

The Built to Run SOP framework: how to document processes in a way that is clear, maintainable, and adopted by your team.

05

The Delegation Framework

Move from doing to directing. Learn the four levels of delegation and how to hand off outcomes, not just tasks.

06

Hiring for the System

Recruit people who thrive in systems-driven environments. Job descriptions, interview frameworks, and evaluation criteria built for operational excellence.

07

Onboarding That Scales

Build an onboarding system that gets new hires productive in days. Checklists, training sequences, and competency milestones.

08

Meeting Architecture

Design a meeting cadence that keeps everyone aligned without wasting time. Daily standups, weekly reviews, monthly strategy, quarterly planning.

09

Metrics, KPIs, and Dashboards

Identify the numbers that matter, build dashboards that surface signal, and manage by exception instead of by presence.

10

Communication Systems

Internal communication frameworks that reduce noise and increase clarity. Async defaults, escalation paths, and decision logs.

11

Quality Control Without Micromanagement

Build quality into the system, not into your personal oversight. Checklists, peer review, automated checks, and exception reporting.

12

Automation and Technology

What to automate, what to delegate, and what to eliminate. A practical guide to choosing and implementing the right tools.

13

Financial Systems and Controls

Cash flow dashboards, approval workflows, budget tracking, and financial reporting systems that keep you informed without being in the weeds.

14

Scaling the Machine

Once the system works, learn how to scale it. Adding locations, products, teams, or markets without losing operational integrity.

15

The Owner's Exit

What it looks like to step back. Transition plans, leadership development, and building a business that someone else can run or buy.
