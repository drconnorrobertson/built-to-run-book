What is Built to Run about?

Built to Run focuses on business systems and operations that can function beyond the founder. Its companion library covers SOPs, delegation, decision authority, management reviews and scorecards. AI resources extend the website learning material with practical preparation and documentation workflows.

How can a business run without the owner handling everything?

Define roles, document repeatable work, assign decision authority and establish measurable reviews. Build management capacity before withdrawing from daily decisions. Independence means a team can execute routine work and escalate the right issues, not that ownership has no responsibilities.

What is a founder bottleneck audit?

List recurring requests that wait for the founder. Classify them as missing instructions, unclear authority, insufficient capability or decisions that genuinely require leadership. Improve the most frequent category first. Review actual completed work to see whether the change reduced waiting.

How do you write useful SOPs?

Observe a real task, define its trigger and output, record steps and exception handling, then test the instructions with another employee. Publish the approved procedure with an owner and revision date. AI can help organize the draft from an authorized walkthrough.

What belongs on a weekly business scorecard?

Choose a small group of decision-relevant metrics for demand, delivery, cash and capacity. Define the source, counting rule, cutoff and owner for each. Compare actual with target and prior period, then record actions against the gaps managers can influence.

How should delegation work?

Give the employee a clear outcome, access to required resources and explicit authority. Define what they can decide and what requires approval. Review the finished result and coach recurring judgment points. Delegation becomes sustainable when routine instructions no longer live only in the founder memory.

What is a decision authority matrix?

It maps decisions to roles and approval boundaries. Include who prepares the information, who approves and who executes. Use actual authority rather than job titles alone. Update the matrix when responsibilities or spending limits change.

How can AI help build business systems?

AI can draft SOPs from walkthroughs, organize metric definitions, prepare meeting briefs and summarize authorized operating records. Start with one process, identify the accountable owner and test the output. Expand only after the team can maintain the workflow and review its quality.

How do you build a management team?

Define the operating outcomes each role owns, recruit for those responsibilities and provide reliable information and authority. Use consistent reviews to identify support needs. A title without decision rights or measurement does not remove dependence on the founder.

How can an owner prepare for time away?

Test coverage for approvals, customer issues, payroll, cash monitoring and urgent operational decisions. Assign temporary authority and escalation routes. Try a limited period away, review what still required founder involvement and improve those handoffs before extending the test.
