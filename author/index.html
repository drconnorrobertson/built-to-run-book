About the Author

Meet Dr. Connor Robertson, the mind behind Built to Run.

Dr. Connor Robertson

Author, Entrepreneur, Systems Builder

Dr. Connor Robertson is an entrepreneur, advisor, and systems thinker who has spent his career building businesses that operate independently of any single person. His approach combines academic rigor with real-world execution, creating frameworks that founders can implement immediately.

With experience spanning tax advisory, media, real estate, and technology, Connor has built and systemized multiple businesses from the ground up. He understands the unique challenge of being the founder who wants to step back but does not know how.

Built to Run distills everything he has learned about operations, delegation, and scaling into a single, actionable playbook. It is the book he wished existed when he started his first company.

Connor is based in Pittsburgh, PA, where he also publishes The Pittsburgh Wire and runs AE Tax Advisors.
